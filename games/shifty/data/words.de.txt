# shifty words (German): Krakel's list (games/krakel/data/words.de.txt),
# taken over as it is (docs/shifty-design.md, "Words"). A round puts
# sixteen words of one category on the board, one of them the secret.
# The words of a category are close (Fuchs, Wolf, Schakal), so a clue
# has to be precise, and no word or other name of a category says
# another (the same stem, or inside a longer word from four letters;
# folded: ä → a, ß → ss), so a clue that says one board word is
# refused as that word, never as another. `== Titel` starts a category
# (at most 26 characters, at least 20 words); one entry per line, the
# word first, then `|` and the other words people write for the same
# thing (a clue saying any of them is refused). `node scripts/words.ts`
# checks the list.
# Lines starting with # are comments; blank lines are ignored.
