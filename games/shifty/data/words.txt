# shifty words (English): Krakel's list (games/krakel/data/words.txt),
# taken over as it is (docs/shifty-design.md, "Words"). A round puts
# sixteen words of one category on the board, one of them the secret.
# The words of a category are close (fox, wolf, jackal), so a clue has
# to be precise, and no word or other name of a category says another
# (the same stem, or inside a longer word from four letters), so a clue
# that says one board word is refused as that word, never as another.
# `== Title` starts a category (at most 26 characters, at least 20
# words); one entry per line, the word first, then `|` and the other
# words people write for the same thing (a clue saying any of them is
# refused). `node scripts/words.ts` checks the list.
# Lines starting with # are comments; blank lines are ignored.
