# drawbluff's prompts in English (written for drawbluff), fetched by the
# host page (data/prompts.de.txt: German, written for German speakers,
# not translated).
#
# Format: `[tag]` starts a structure, then one prompt per line, its
# three decoys after it, each after ` | `; blank lines and `#` comments
# are skipped. A round mixes the structures (no structure twice among
# eight prompts in a row, `deck` in game/rules.ts). A room deals no
# prompt twice until it dealt them all (`PromptDeck`).
#
# Decoys are the lies the game adds when a drawing has too few: other
# titles for the drawing a player would make of the prompt (a thing
# swapped for one that looks alike, the picture told as another story),
# at least two in the prompt's structure, so the truth is not the only
# one that sounds like a prompt; never the prompt reworded, never a
# prompt of the file, none twice in the file.
#
# Every prompt is in its display form (lowercase, letters, spaces and
# apostrophes, nothing at the end) and at most 40 characters, the
# longest lie a phone takes (LIE_SOFT), so neither form nor length is a
# tell; so is every decoy. Drawable, family-friendly, our own; no two
# alike (`node scripts/prompts.ts --near` lists near duplicates;
# test/prompts.test.ts checks all of it).
#
# Prompts and decoys are written in the forms players type their lies
# in, so that neither the truth nor the decoys look like the file's:
# about a third with a leading article ("a vampire at the dentist"), the
# rest without ("vampire at the dentist"), in plural ("vampires at the
# dentist"), as a short label ("sweaty snowman"), a sentence ("the
# snowman is melting", "grandma discovers emoji"), "when …", "my …",
# telegraphic ("cat doing taxes"); short and long ones. A prompt is in
# the forms of its decoys, and at least one decoy shares whether it
# starts with an article (`node scripts/prompts.ts` measures the forms,
# the lints keep both alike).
#
# The structures:
#   odd       odd combinations: lonely lighthouse at a party
#   idiom     idioms taken literally: raining cats and dogs
#   feeling   abstract ideas and feelings: fear of mondays
#   moment    the moment when: the moment the ice cream falls
#   secret    the secret lives of things: what my fridge does at night
#   invented  invented jobs, sports, holidays: a puddle inspector
#   contra    misleading or contradictory pairs: shy fireworks show
#   view      an unusual point of view: the world seen by an ant
#   drama     tiny dramas: last cookie standoff
#   title     famous-sounding invented titles: the phantom of the bakery
#   animal    animals with human problems: cat filing its taxes
#   food      food with a life of its own: soup that fights back
#   place     places that should not exist: a school for baby dragons
#   gadget    inventions nobody needs: hat that waters your plants
#   power     useless superpowers: sneezing glitter
#   then      history gets mixed up: caveman with a selfie stick
#   scene     everyday scenes gone wrong: grandpa's first video call
