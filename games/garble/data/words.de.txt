# garble words (German): what a layer has to become.
# One entry per line: the word first (a concrete thing you can draw,
# without article, singular), then `|` and the other words people write
# for it (synonyms, irregular plurals, the everyday name): a guess saying
# any of them reads the layer right (game/rules.ts, judge); spelling
# slips, ae/oe/ue for umlauts and regular plurals are the matcher's job.
# No word or synonym says another entry's (inside a longer word from
# four letters, or with the same stem; folded: ä → a, ß → ss):
# test/rules.test.ts checks it. That keeps a few out (Fledermaus has a
# Maus, Spiegel an Igel, Haifisch a Fisch).
# Lines starting with # are comments; blank lines are ignored.
