# Word list credits

The secret word list of Jinx (`words.txt`) was selected and curated by the
Jinx authors. No data from the sources below is included in the game;
they were used to choose the words (how well known, how concrete, how
often children meet them, part of speech, spelling). We thank their authors.

- **Lancaster Sensorimotor Norms** — Lynott, D., Connell, L., Brysbaert, M.,
  Brand, J., & Carney, J. (2020). The Lancaster Sensorimotor Norms:
  multidimensional measures of perceptual and action strength for 40,000
  English words. *Behavior Research Methods, 52*, 1271–1291.
  https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-019-01316-z — data: https://osf.io/7emr6/ —
  licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- **The Glasgow Norms** — Scott, G. G., Keitel, A., Becirspahic, M., Yao, B.,
  & Sereno, S. C. (2019). The Glasgow Norms: Ratings of 5,500 words on nine
  scales. *Behavior Research Methods, 51*, 1258–1270.
  https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-018-1099-3 — data: https://osf.io/42s65/ —
  licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- **CYP-LEX: The Children and Young People's Books Lexicon** — Korochkina,
  M., Marelli, M., Brysbaert, M., & Rastle, K. (2024). *Quarterly Journal of
  Experimental Psychology*. https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218241229694 —
  data: https://osf.io/squ49/ — licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). CYP-LEX also
  carries frequencies from SUBTLEX-UK (van Heuven, W. J. B., Mandera, P.,
  Keuleers, E., & Brysbaert, M., 2014, *QJEP 67*, 1176–1190).

- **Open English WordNet** (2025 edition) — the Open English WordNet
  Community, https://en-word.net/ — licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Derived from
  Princeton WordNet 3.0, © 2006 Princeton University.

- **SCOWL** (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists, 2020.12.07) and VarCon —
  http://wordlist.aspell.net/

# German

The German secret word list (`words.de.txt`) was written by the Jinx
authors; no data is included from the dictionaries it was spell-checked
against (igerman98 Hunspell dictionaries for Germany, Austria and
Switzerland).

The German lexicon (`dict.de.txt`), which the game uses to tell whether
two clues are the same word, **is** included. It is derived from the
**German Wiktionary** (https://de.wiktionary.org/, extracted by
wiktextract, https://kaikki.org/dewiktionary/) and licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), like the
Wiktionary; its authors are listed in the history of each entry. See
`dict.de-LICENSE.txt`. Which words it holds was chosen with word
frequencies from OpenSubtitles 2018 (Hermit Dave's FrequencyWords,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, CC BY-SA 4.0), which are not
included.
