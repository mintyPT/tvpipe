    Chromecast from Chrome or Edge
    1. Pick A second window and Start.
    2. In the new window, open the browser's menu (⋮), then Cast…; under Sources choose Cast tab and pick the Chromecast.
    3. The TV runs about a second behind the phones: fine for drawing and word games, less for quick ones.
    Casting without delay

    The tvpipe app on a computer casts to Apple TV and Chromecast itself, about a quarter of a second behind. Install it, then tvpipe game play.
