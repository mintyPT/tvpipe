<h1><svg viewBox="0 0 560 520" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#logo"/></svg><span>tv<b>pipe</b></span></h1>

tvpipe puts video and sound from your computer on a TV. It plays files and URLs, mirrors cameras, browser tabs and web pages, and sends them to Apple TV, Chromecast and anything else that speaks AirPlay or Google Cast. It is one command line tool for macOS, Linux and Windows.

[Read the manual](manual/overview.md)
[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/mitsuhiko/tvpipe)
:::

:::install
:::

:::features
### Google Cast
Chromecast, Google TV and TVs with Cast built in.

### AirPlay
Apple TV and TVs with AirPlay built in.

### Browsers
A browser tab can be a receiver too, for previews or as an OBS source.

### macOS, Linux, Windows
The same tool and the same commands on all three.

### Videos and websites
Files, URLs, cameras, the screen, a browser tab or any web page.
:::

<h2 class="lh">Party games</h2>

Games on the TV that everyone plays on their own phone: scan the code, type a name, play. `tvpipe game play` downloads one and starts it. [All the games](games/)

No tvpipe at hand? The games also run in a browser, on a laptop or an iPad: [play in the browser](/games/).
